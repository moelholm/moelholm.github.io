### Nano Summary ⚡️

I finished in `17:47:18` — a `23-minute` PB compared to last year (`18:11:00`) 🥳🚀! It wasn't all smooth sailing though: the weather was fine until late afternoon/early evening when the rain started pouring down 🌧️😒, and combined with a hard muscular bonk around `110–120K`, I got seriously cooled down 🥶.

Thankfully, the amazing volunteers at aid station 24 (`122K`) took incredible care of me with dry clothes, blankets, food, a few minutes in a chair, and a warm cup of coffee ☕️🙏🏻 — a complete reset that got me moving well again. Later, around `02:30 AM` outside Jesperhus, my awesome colleague Andreas surprised me in the middle of the night for cheering and "Nicky in ultra" selfies 🤠📸🙏🏻!

### Recommended? 👏🏻

Absolutely yes 🤩.

Mors 100 Miles is a truly extraordinary race where the entire island comes together to celebrate and support the runners from start to finish 🥳. With an aid station roughly every 5 kilometers and volunteers who go above and beyond — especially when the weather turns rough in the middle of the night — you are in the best possible hands all 160 kilometers around the island 👏🏻🤗.

This one gets my top rating: ⭐️⭐️⭐️⭐️⭐️
