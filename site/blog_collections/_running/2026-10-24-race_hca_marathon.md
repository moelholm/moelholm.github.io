### Nano Summary ⚡️

I gambled 🎲😂. The day before, Garmin had my training readiness at "Poor", status "Strained" and recovery at "High Need" — but I still optimistically lined up with the 3-hour balloons 🎈. That went fine through the first half (`1:30:07`)… well, honestly until around `18–19K`. Then my power just decayed exponentially, cramps were knocking before `30K`, and the rest was pure damage control 😇🫣. Not recovered enough from [the 100 miler](/blog/running/2026/10/24/race_mors_100_miles), I think — lesson learned 🙃.

The race itself was excellent though 💯🥳: perfect weather, spectators cheering all around, musicians, DJs and choirs, tunnels and the city center — never boring. I suffered a lot and struggled to truly appreciate the moment, but I finished in `{{ page.duration_formatted }}` and I'm still happy and proud 🤠.

### Recommended? 👏🏻

Absolutely yes 🤩.

HCA Marathon is a lovely city marathon with a great route through Odense, a perfectly run organization, and a fantastic atmosphere all the way around 🥳. Add a nice t-shirt and medal on top, and it's an easy recommendation — just show up a bit more recovered than I did 😅.

This one gets my top rating: ⭐️⭐️⭐️⭐️⭐️
