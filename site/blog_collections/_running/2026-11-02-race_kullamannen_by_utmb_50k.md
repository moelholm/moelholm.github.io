### Nano Summary ⚡️

I had mixed feelings going in 😟😅: part of me felt like I was missing out on the "real" tough 100 mile experience. But choosing the 50K let me split my training with serious Lifting+WOD, and going all in on a faster race was a blast 🔥. Old boy got punished for it though 😂: severe hamstring cramps forced me to full stops several times, I slipped big time in the mud, and I fell down some stairs (3–4 steps), emptying all the air in my lungs 🤕. And AGAIN the evil beach segments with stone hell took out all my speed 🪨 — but this year I kept running (well, jogging 😎) through them. I finished in `{{ page.duration_formatted }}` — and it was so nice not being completely smashed like the previous years 🥳.

Casper, my son, ran the kids race while I was out on the course, so we both got medals 🏅🏅 (I actually like this year's medal a lot 🤩). The family cheered for me — though they missed me at Hovs Hallar (too fast while they were shopping 😂), and only my daughter made it to the finish line in time. My wife now says I'm not allowed to run the 50K anymore, because I'm too fast for her to do her Sweden things 😆.

### Recommended? 👏🏻

Absolutely yes 🤩.

Kullamannen is a superbly organized UTMB event with a fantastic route along the Kullaberg coast — forest trails, cliffs, mud, stairs and those brutal stony beaches 🌊. The 50K gives you the full Kullamannen flavour in a single (long) day, and it's a perfect family trip too, with a kids race and plenty of Sweden things to do while you're out running 🇸🇪.

This one gets my top rating: ⭐️⭐️⭐️⭐️⭐️
