Downgraded Kullamannen : 100M ➡️ 50K

I decided to “downgrade” my race this year. And the awesome Kullamannen race org changed it for me today 🤩🙇‍♂️

Q: Why downgrade? 

A: Right now I don’t feel excited for the outlook to a ~24 hour brutal run. Also, the last 2 years I’ve envied the 50K runners for their fresh strong running, while I was walk-jogging at best. I want that experience now 🤠👌🏻

A 50K also disrupts training less: which I value currently.
